LAUNCH

A real ball, delivered at a chosen pace and spin. Nothing on the player, nothing on the racquet.

02
TRACK

Bracket-mounted cameras flanking the screen hold the ball through the full path of the rally.

03
MEASURE

Speed, launch angle, apex and landing, shot by shot — no wearable, no marker, no modified ball.

04

A closed loop — balls travel the ramp to an auger at the screen base and feed straight back. Nobody picks up balls.

Why the footprint matters

FOUR BAYS FIT
IN ONE COURT

A court sells time to four players, outdoors, when the weather allows. The same floor, divided into bays, sells four bookable stations, every hour, every month of the year — each one metering its own sessions and its own bar tab. The square footage doesn't change. What it earns per square foot does.
